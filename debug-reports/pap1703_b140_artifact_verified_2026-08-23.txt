NOT PROVEN HERE — and not provable from this host
-------------------------------------------------
AC3 asks for >=10x on device. Everything above proves the native library SHIPS
and CAN load. It does not prove it DOES load on the FP5, nor how fast it is.
That needs stageMs.preprocessBackend == 'native-cpp' from a real photo session.
No adb, no emulator, no device in this sandbox.

Known risk on the >=10x bar (flagged before the session, not after):
host JS preprocess mean 106.2ms -> host native 66.2ms = 1.60x
(debug-reports/pap1694_native_parity.json, n=431, byte-identical 431/431).
Host JS is V8-JIT'd so C++ has little headroom there. The >=10x bar rests
entirely on the device's Hermes penalty (~33-38x, project_PAP1682_*), which is
what makes the device JS baseline ~2.9-3.0s. A 7x or 8x device result would be
a real measurement, not a broken build.
